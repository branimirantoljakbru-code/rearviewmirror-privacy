Rearviewmirror – Delete Your Account

Rearviewmirror allows users to delete their account directly from the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to Rearviewmirror.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your Rearviewmirror account and the personal content associated with it, including memories and related user-created data.
